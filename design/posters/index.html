02What a certificate says

A minimum.Never an amount.

It proves

  1. The disclosed notes exist in the Rings tree and were unspent at the slot it names.
  2. Together they reach the tier: at least 1,000 SOL means at least 1,000 SOL.
  3. The subject signed for this vault, so the tier is bound to it.
  4. It is still true now: a spend revokes it on chain.

It does not prove

  1. How much you hold in total, or anything about the rest of your Ring.
  2. That the money was never borrowed. Revocation catches the return, not the loan.
  3. Anything about the past. It starts at the slot it was certified.
  4. Anonymity. A wallet subject names the wallet; choose a badge to name nobody.

03How it works

Four steps, one signature you can read.

  1. 01

    Shield

    Move SOL or USDC into your own POF vault, a separate Privacy Ring made from one wallet signature. The money stays yours and stays shielded.

  2. 02

    Choose

    Pick a tier, from at least 1 SOL to at least 10,000, and a subject: this wallet, an X handle, or a fresh badge that names nobody.

  3. 03

    Prove

    Your browser picks the fewest notes that reach the tier. The Verifier checks they exist and are unspent, keeps nothing, and signs. The chain stores the certificate.

  4. 04

    Stay live

    The keeper watches the notes behind every certificate. Spend one and the certificate is revoked on chain, with the slot, for everyone at once.

04Revocation

Move the money,the certificatebreaks.

The keeper watches the notes behind every certificate. Spend one and the certificate is revoked on chain, with the slot, for everyone at once.

When a disclosed note is spent, the keeper proves it on chain and the card turns over. Nobody can edit a card, including us.
