AThe sequence

From a shielded note to a public certificate, in five moves.

  1. 01

    Shield

    Move SOL or USDC into your own POF vault, a separate Privacy Ring made from one wallet signature. The money stays yours and stays shielded.

  2. 02

    Disclose

    Your browser picks the fewest notes that reach the tier and opens only those to the Verifier, for one request. The rest of the ring stays closed.

  3. 03

    Attest

    The Verifier checks the notes exist and are unspent, keeps nothing, and signs a fixed 240-byte attestation. The public log records every one.

  4. 04

    Certify

    The program rebuilds the 240 bytes, checks the Verifier's signature against the key its Config trusts, and writes the certificate on chain.

  5. 05

    Revoke

    Spend a disclosed note and anyone holding its revocation pair proves it from chain state. The certificate is revoked at that slot, for everyone.
