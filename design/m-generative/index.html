02 How it works

Five steps, from a shielded note to a certificate that breaks.

  1. 01 Shield

    Move SOL or USDC into your own POF vault, a separate Privacy Ring made from one wallet signature. The money stays yours and stays shielded.

    Every note is drawn the same size. The amounts never show.

  2. 02 Disclose

    Your browser picks the fewest shielded notes that reach the tier, from 1 to 8, and sends only their openings to the Verifier.

    Three notes reach the tier. The rest of the Ring stays dark.

  3. 03 Attest

    The Verifier checks each commitment is in the Rings state tree and each nullifier is unspent, checks the sum reaches the tier, signs 240 bytes, and keeps nothing.

    A leaf climbs its path to a root the tree account holds.

  4. 04 Certify

    The pof program rebuilds those 240 bytes on chain, checks the Verifier's ed25519 signature, and writes the certificate. The transparency log records the attestation.

    240 ticks, one per byte. The mark is drawn inside them.

  5. 05 Revoke

    The keeper watches the disclosed nullifiers. Spend one and the certificate is revoked on chain, with the slot, for everyone at once. You hold the same pairs, so it never depends on us.

    One nullifier appears on chain. The mark comes apart.

03 The mark

Every certificate draws its own mark.

The 32 bytes of the subject hash become eight rings of 32 bits: a one is a drawn arc, a zero is a gap. The same subject always draws the same mark. Move the money and it comes apart, on chain and on this page.

05 What a certificate says

A minimum. Never an amount.

It proves

  • The disclosed notes exist in the Rings tree and were unspent at the slot it names.
  • Together they reach the tier: at least 1,000 SOL means at least 1,000 SOL.
  • The subject signed for this vault, so the tier is bound to it.
  • It is still true now: a spend revokes it on chain.

It does not prove

  • How much you hold in total, or anything about the rest of your Ring.
  • That the money was never borrowed. Revocation catches the return, not the loan.
  • Anything about the past. It starts at the slot it was certified.
  • Anonymity. A wallet subject names the wallet; choose a badge to name nobody.
