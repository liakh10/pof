01TiersEach ring is a tier

A minimum. Never an amount.

Pick a tier, from at least 1 SOL to at least 10,000, or from at least 100 USDC to at least 1,000,000. Each ring of the dial is one tier: it lights in proportion to the live certificates at that tier, read from chain. The hand moves one tick per slot.

03ProcedureFour steps, one signature you can read

How it works.

StepActionWho actsWhat happens
01ShieldYour walletMove SOL or USDC into your own POF vault, a separate Privacy Ring made from one wallet signature. The money stays yours and stays shielded.
02ChooseYouPick a tier, from at least 1 SOL to at least 10,000, and a subject: this wallet, an X handle, or a fresh badge that names nobody.
03ProveYour browser, the Verifier, the chainYour browser picks the fewest notes that reach the tier. The Verifier checks they exist and are unspent, keeps nothing, and signs. The chain stores the certificate.
04Stay liveThe keeperThe keeper watches the notes behind every certificate. Spend one and the certificate is revoked on chain, with the slot, for everyone at once.

04SpecificationWhat a certificate says

It proves a minimum, and nothing else.

It proves

  1. P.1The disclosed notes exist in the Rings tree and were unspent at the slot it names.
  2. P.2Together they reach the tier: at least 1,000 SOL means at least 1,000 SOL.
  3. P.3The subject signed for this vault, so the tier is bound to it.
  4. P.4It is still true now: a spend revokes it on chain.

It does not prove

  1. N.1How much you hold in total, or anything about the rest of your Ring.
  2. N.2That the money was never borrowed. Revocation catches the return, not the loan.
  3. N.3Anything about the past. It starts at the slot it was certified.
  4. N.4Anonymity. A wallet subject names the wallet; choose a badge to name nobody.
