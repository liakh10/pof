Guides

Plain, step-by-step instructions. Each one links to the part of the paper that explains why.

If you hold funds

  1. Get your first certificate with Phantom: the vault, shielding, the wait, the subject, paying, and backing up the kit.
  2. Prove an X handle: one public post links a handle you control.
  3. Revoke, renew, and moving your funds: how a certificate ends, and the order of steps that keeps a handle private.

If you check other people's funds 4. Verify someone's proof: on the site, through the API, from a terminal, or with a badge on your page. 5. Require a certificate before a deal: for OTC desks and counterparties, including an on-chain check.

If you run a community 6. Gate a Telegram group or a Discord role.

Every wait in these guides is counted in slots, the chain's own clock (a slot is about 0.4 seconds). The messages quoted in bold are the exact sentences the site shows.
