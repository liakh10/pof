Post proof, not wallet. Why a proof of funds is a standard document in finance, and what changes when the funds sit in a shielded pool.

Motivation

Post proof, not wallet#

On public chains the usual way to show you can pay is to show the wallet. That proves the balance and gives away everything else: the full history, every counterparty, the next move. Traders, funds and people who negotiate deals keep being asked to make that trade.

A shielded pool changes the default. Since Helius Privacy Rings went live, amounts and assets inside a ring are encrypted; addresses on the default ring stay visible. Inside a ring, a balance is no longer public, and so it can no longer be shown by pointing at it. The question becomes how to prove a fact about it, "at least this much", without reopening the rest.

A document finance already uses#

A proof of funds letter is a routine instrument: a bank states that a client holds at least a sum, for a counterparty who needs to know the client can complete a deal (property, acquisitions, OTC trades, sponsorships, accredited-investor checks). The letter names a figure or a floor, a date and the issuer. It does not hand over the statement.

POF keeps the useful properties of that letter and removes the weak ones:

a bank lettera POF certificate
What it statesholds at least X on a dateholds at least a tier, live at the current slot
Who vouchesthe bankthe Verifier's signature, checked by the program, recorded in a public log
What the reader seesthe lettera chain account anyone can read
When it goes stalesilently, the day afteron chain, at the slot a disclosed note is spent (once the spend is shown)
How a reader checks itcalls the bankreads the account, or the API, or the SDK
Forgerya convincing lettera picture of a card; the account is the only source of truth

Why a live floor and not an amount#

A floor is what a counterparty needs and the least a holder must give away. Tiers are coarse by design (powers of ten: 1 to 10,000 SOL, 100 to 1,000,000 USDC) so that a tier says little beyond what it is asked to say. The certificate never stores an amount; the Verifier compares the sum with the tier and returns only yes or no.

A floor that cannot die is not worth much on a chain where money moves in one slot. So a POF certificate binds the exact notes it was built from, by salted hashes of their nullifiers, and the program accepts a proof that one of them was spent from the Rings program's own accounts. Moving the money is what ends the certificate.

Who uses it on day one#

  • People who post under a handle prove size without tying the handle to a wallet.
  • Group admins gate a Telegram or Discord community to holders of a live tier, through on-chain Rooms and the bots.
  • Counterparties in OTC trades and sponsorships check a proof of funds before a deal.
  • Programs gate an instruction with one CPI, require_certificate(subject_hash, asset, min_tier).
  • Agents ask the MCP server whether a subject holds a live certificate.

What POF is not#

  • Not a statement of total wealth: only the disclosed notes are seen.
  • Not a statement of ownership: control of a ring is what is shown (Trust points, item 6).
  • Not anonymity: the subject is public, and so is the way you funded the ring (Privacy guidance).
  • Not a custodian: POF never holds your funds or your spend key. Every spend is signed by the ring owner's own key.
