Glossary

Anchor. A signed tree head of the transparency log submitted to the program's anchor_log; its size, root and slot are kept in the Treasury. The log must always extend the anchored head.

Attestation. The 240 bytes the Verifier signs with ed25519 (CONTRACT §3), rebuilt and checked on chain by certify.

Badge. A subject kind (2): a fresh ed25519 key made for one certificate, linked to nothing else.

Bundle hash. sha256 of the Verifier's deterministic build (verifier/dist/pof-verifier.mjs), carried in every log entry.

Certificate. The program account ["cert", subject_hash, [asset]]: the tier, status, slots and salted nullifier hashes for one subject and one asset.

Commitment. The Poseidon hash that is a note's leaf in the Rings state tree, computed from its asset, amount, owner and blinding.

Controller. The key that may revoke a certificate and join Rooms with it: the wallet, the badge key, or a key derived from the vault for an X handle.

Deposit (shield). Moving SOL or USDC from a public balance into a ring. Public: depositor, amount and recipient.

Disclosure package. The JSON the browser sends to the Verifier (CONTRACT §4): the subject proof, the ring owner and nk, and the openings of the chosen notes.

Expiry slot. The slot after which a certificate reads as expired; at most validity_slots after its issue slot.

Indexer. Helius's service for Rings data: Merkle paths, non-inclusion paths, transactions. The Verifier re-checks its paths against on-chain roots.

Keeper. A service that holds the revoke pairs (escrowed to its X25519 key), watches the disclosed nullifiers and calls revoke_spent. Registered keepers are listed in verifier/keepers.json; the project's keeper also anchors the log.

Kit (revoke kit). For one certificate: each disclosed note's salt and nullifier, plus sealed openings. Kept by the holder and the keeper.

Leaf (log). The 377 bytes of one transparency log entry (CONTRACT §7).

Live. Status byte live and the current slot at most the expiry slot.

nk (nullifier key). A ring's 31-byte secret that derives its notes' nullifiers. Cannot spend; never rotates.

Note. A shielded amount of one asset in a ring: one leaf of the Rings state tree.

Nullifier. Poseidon(commitment, blinding, nk). Published by the Rings program, as a PDA, when the note is spent.

Nullifier PDA. ["nullifier", tree, nullifier] under the Rings pool program: 11 bytes, first byte 8. Its existence proves the spend.

Opening. What a note's commitment is computed from: asset, amount, blinding, leaf index, optional data fields.

Payer. The key that pays the fee and the rent of a certificate: the wallet, or the relayer.

Privacy Ring (ring). A Helius shielded account space owned by an Ed25519 key; amounts and assets inside are encrypted.

Relayer. The key that submits certify for X-handle and badge subjects after the holder pays it.

Renew. A fresh attestation over the same notes, subject and controller; extends a live certificate.

Room. A pof_gate account with an asset and a minimum tier; members join with a live certificate.

Salt / salted nullifier hash. A random 32 bytes per disclosed note; the certificate stores sha256("pof:null:" ‖ salt ‖ nullifier), so the nullifier stays hidden until it is shown.

Signed tree head (STH). The Verifier's signature over "POF-STH1" ‖ program id ‖ size ‖ root.

Slot. Solana's unit of time and the only clock in POF.

State root. The root of the Rings state tree; the tree account keeps the last 500.

Subject. What a certificate is about: a wallet (0), an X handle (1) or a badge (2), stored as a hash (CONTRACT §1).

Tier. A floor in a fixed table: SOL 1, 10, 100, 1,000, 10,000; USDC 100 to 1,000,000.

Transparency log. The append-only Merkle log of every attestation, RFC 9162 hashing.

Vault. The ring the client certifies from, separate from the wallet's own ring. Its spending key is derived from one wallet signature over the vault message (or is random, with a passphrase backup, for wallets that do not sign deterministically).

Verifier. The off-chain service that checks a disclosure package and signs the attestation. Keeps no amount, opening or nk.

Witness. An independent monitor of the transparency log that checks each signed head against the chain and the heads before it, cosigns it, and records alarms (CONTRACT §8).
