Fees and the $POF holder rule

What a certificate costs#

The admin can change the fee with set_fee or set_params, but never above 250,000,000 lamports (0.25 SOL): the cap is a constant in the program (FEE_CAP). Every change emits a ConfigSet event.

Revocation and expiry cost only the network fee of whoever sends them. Joining a Room costs the member account's rent, returned when the member leaves or is evicted.

Who pays no protocol fee#

Holders of $POF. If the payer's canonical associated token account for the root mint (the $POF mint, called root_mint in Config) holds at least holder_min (1,000,000 $POF at launch) at the moment of certify or renew, the program takes no fee and counts the call as exempt in the Treasury. The check reads that one account, owned by the payer, of that mint; tokens held elsewhere do not count. Until the root mint is set in Config, nobody is exempt.

Relayed certificates. When the relayer is the payer, the program charges no on-chain fee and counts the call as relayed: the holder already paid the relayer. The relayer's quote is the Config fee, the certificate rent, a 10,000-lamport network allowance and a unique tag of 1 to 99,999 lamports that identifies the payment, all in one transfer. The rent of a relayed certificate returns to the relayer at expire.

The holder rule applies to the payer, so it applies when you submit certify yourself. A handle or badge certificate submitted from your own wallet as payer gets the exemption, and names that wallet on chain as the payer: you choose between the fee and that link (Privacy guidance).

Where the fees go#

The Treasury holds the fees above its own rent and publishes its counters: fees_in, fees_out, certificates certified, renewed, revoked by spend, revoked by holder, expired, live, the sum of live tier minimums by asset, relayed and exempt calls, and the anchored log head. withdraw_fees sends the surplus to the dev key named in Config (and only the surplus: the Treasury's rent stays). Each withdrawal emits FeesWithdrawn with the amount.

The $POF token#

$POF is the protocol's token. It is created as a plain pump.fun token; its creator fees go to the dev wallet. Its only role in the protocol is the holder rule above. Its address is published on the site and in this repository's config once it exists, and not before.
