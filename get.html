Six steps in this page. Your wallet signs three or four times; the vault key signs in the page. The amounts never leave your browser except to the Verifier, which keeps none of them.

Certificates open at launch.

You can connect and make your vault now: that is one signature and nothing goes on chain. Registering, shielding and certifying turn on when the program is live.

01 Wallet

Not connected

The wallet pays the network fees and, for a wallet certificate, is the subject. For an X handle or a badge it never appears on the certificate.

02 Vault

Not made

Your POF vault is a separate Privacy Ring made from one signature of your wallet. Only the vault's keys are ever shown to the Verifier, so your main Ring stays private. The same wallet rebuilds the same vault on any device.

Sign this only on this site.

The vault message says so in its first lines. Whoever gets that signature can move the money in the vault.

03 Shield

A shield moves SOL or USDC from your wallet into the vault. The deposit itself is public; what happens after it is not. Shield once, then let the notes age: a certificate right after a shield is easy to pair with it.

The relayer

For an X handle or a badge, the relayer pays and submits the certificate, so no wallet appears on it. You pay the relayer from the vault with a private transfer. The vault key pays that transfer's network fee itself, so it needs a little SOL of its own.
