Test report: phase 2, the zero-knowledge proof of funds (zk/)

Development setup only. Every number and every proof below comes from a trusted setup made on one machine (zk/setup/TRANSCRIPT.md). Whoever ran it could forge proofs for that key. It is fit for local tests and devnet, never for mainnet certificates; the public ceremony that replaces it is in docs-src/zk.md. certify_zk is not deployed anywhere.

Scope: zk/circuits/pof.circom (the circuit), zk/js/zk.js (inputs, context, escrow, proof encoding, the prover call), zk/scripts/ (build, ceremony, verifying key to Rust), zk/bench/ (proving in the browser), zk/onchain/ (the Groth16 verifier over the alt_bn128 syscalls and the certify_zk patch for pof, applied by apply.py to a copy of program/). Node 20, circom 2.2.2, snarkjs 0.7.5, circomlib 2.0.5, solana-cli 4.1.2 (Agave), Anchor 1.2.0, solana-bn254 3.2.1. No mainnet transaction was sent. Mainnet was read for one finalized blockhash per ceremony phase (the beacons).

PENDING: filled in when the runs finish.
