Rooms

Rooms that check the money at the door, and keep checking.

A Room is an account of the pof_gate program with a minimum tier. Joining writes a member account only if your certificate is live and high enough. When it dies, anyone can evict you. Telegram groups and Discord roles follow the Room.

Open rooms

On chain now

Create a room

You pay the room's rent (a few thousandths of a SOL, returned if you close it). The tier is fixed per room; members of a lower tier cannot join.
