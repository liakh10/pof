Verify

Check anyone. It takes one read.

Type a wallet, an @handle or a badge key. The answer comes from the chain, with the slot it was read at. Add a minimum to get a plain yes or no.

From your own code

sh
curl https:///v1/certificates/@anon?asset=SOL&minTier=4

What a yes means

A live certificate of that asset at that tier or higher: the notes behind it were in the Rings tree and unspent when the Verifier signed, and none has been spent since, or the keeper would have revoked it. It never says how much more there is.
