Transparency log

Every signature the Verifier makes, in one append-only log.

Each attestation is a leaf of an RFC 9162 Merkle tree. The head is signed and anchored on chain, so the Verifier cannot sign for one person and hide it from everyone else. No amounts, no openings, no nullifiers: only the 240 signed bytes.
