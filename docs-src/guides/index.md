# Guides

Plain, step-by-step instructions. Each one links to the part of the paper that explains why.

**If you hold funds**
1. [Get your first certificate with Phantom](first-certificate): the vault, shielding, the wait, the subject, paying,
   and backing up the kit.
2. [Prove an X handle](prove-x-handle): one public post links a handle you control.
3. [Revoke, renew, and moving your funds](revoke-renew-move): how a certificate ends, and the order of steps that keeps
   a handle private.

**If you check other people's funds**
4. [Verify someone's proof](verify-a-proof): on the site, through the API, from a terminal, or with a badge on your page.
5. [Require a certificate before a deal](otc-deals): for OTC desks and counterparties, including an on-chain check.

**If you run a community**
6. [Gate a Telegram group or a Discord role](gate-a-community).

Every wait in these guides is counted in **slots**, the chain's own clock (a slot is about 0.4 seconds). The messages
quoted in bold are the exact sentences the site shows.
